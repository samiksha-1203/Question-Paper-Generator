Marks Scheme

Tell the generator exactly how many questions of each marks value you want — e.g. "6 questions of 2 marks, 6 of 5 marks, 4 of 10 marks". Total marks and question count are computed from this, so the paper can never come out over or under what you planned. Set "Offered" higher than "Must attempt" to create a choice block like "Attempt any 2 of the following 3".
